💡 What does this mean?

Score data: Scores use Tom’s Hardware 1080p gaming rankings where exact results are available. “Bridged” values come from older test suites and are scaled through a processor or graphics card tested in both suites. “Estimate” means no comparable result was available. CPU and GPU scores have separate reference hardware, so they are rankings—not equivalent measured FPS. Important: Pressure and balance remain scenario estimates, not measured utilization or predicted FPS; values above 100% mean modeled demand exceeds the score-based nominal capacity. Real-world performance varies with game engine, graphics settings, drivers, RAM, cooling, background applications and other system factors.
