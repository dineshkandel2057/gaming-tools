Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

SystemFit PC ("SystemFit PC", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit and use the SystemFit PC website and how that information is used.

SystemFit PC provides PC planning and hardware-related tools, including tools for estimating PC performance, comparing hardware, planning builds, and understanding computer components.

1. Information We Collect

SystemFit PC does not currently require visitors to create an account or submit personal information in order to use the site's tools.

However, certain technical and usage information may be collected automatically through third-party services used by the website.

This may include information such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate geographic information
  • Referring website or source
  • Interactions with the website
  • Date and time of visits
  • Other technical information normally associated with web requests

2. Google Analytics

SystemFit PC currently uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website and to improve the site's tools, content, navigation, and performance.

Google Analytics may use cookies, identifiers, and similar technologies to collect information about website usage. The information collected through Google Analytics is processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

We use Google Analytics for aggregated and statistical purposes and do not use it to intentionally identify individual visitors.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

You can also review Google's privacy policy here: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Cookies

SystemFit PC may use cookies and similar technologies through third-party services such as Google Analytics.

Cookies are small files stored by your browser that can help websites and services recognize a browser, remember information, and understand how a website is being used.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how some websites or third-party services function.

4. Google Fonts

SystemFit PC uses fonts provided through Google Fonts. When your browser loads these fonts, your browser may connect to Google's servers to retrieve the required font resources.

Google may process technical information associated with these requests. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

5. Advertising

SystemFit PC does not currently display Google AdSense advertisements on the website.

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added to SystemFit PC in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the applicable advertising technologies, cookies, data processing, and user choices before or when those services are introduced.

6. Third-Party Services

SystemFit PC may rely on third-party services to operate, analyze, host, or improve the website. These services may process certain technical information according to their own privacy policies.

The third-party services currently used by the website include Google Analytics and Google Fonts.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website and the information associated with its operation. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

SystemFit PC is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

9. External Links

SystemFit PC may eventually contain links to external websites, products, services, or other resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. You should review the privacy policies of those websites before providing information to them.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services, analytics, advertising, or applicable requirements change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
