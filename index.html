About PCPilot

PCPilot is built to make PC gaming hardware decisions easier to understand. Our tools turn technical information into simple, practical estimates that beginners can use while still giving experienced PC gamers useful details.

⚡ Simple & Practical

Clear results and explanations without forcing you to understand complicated hardware terminology first.

🛠️ Built for PC Gamers

Tools are designed around real gaming decisions such as bottlenecks, FPS, upgrades, power requirements and component comparisons.

📊 Estimates, Not Guesswork

Where a result is an estimate, PCPilot clearly explains the limitations so you can understand what the number actually means.
