Affiliate Disclosure

SystemFit PC currently does not use affiliate links and has no active affiliate relationships. This page explains the policy that will apply if that changes. Any future commercial relationship will be disclosed clearly so visitors can understand when a link may generate revenue for the site.

How affiliate links work

If SystemFit PC joins an affiliate program in the future, some links may be tracked by a merchant or affiliate network. If a visitor buys through an eligible link, SystemFit PC may receive a commission at no additional cost to the visitor, unless the merchant states otherwise. The merchant controls its own pricing, shipping, returns and product availability.

How recommendations are handled

Affiliate relationships should not be treated as proof that a product is the best choice. SystemFit PC's tools and guides are intended to focus on the user's hardware, workload, budget and performance goals. Visitors should still compare current prices, specifications, availability, warranty terms and independent reviews before buying.

Product availability, prices and specifications can change. Any future affiliate link will not guarantee that a product is suitable for every build, and calculator outputs remain estimates rather than promises of real-world performance.

Current commercial relationships

Current status: No affiliate programs or commercial referral relationships are currently active, and SystemFit PC does not currently use affiliate links. If this status changes, this page will be updated to describe the relevant relationship.

Transparency

Affiliate relationships do not change the underlying methodology of the calculators. Estimated results remain estimates, and product specifications should be confirmed with the manufacturer or retailer before purchase.
