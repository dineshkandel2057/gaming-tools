How this calculator works

SystemFit currently uses a transparent performance model rather than pretending every result is a measured benchmark. Each game has a workload profile, while CPUs and GPUs are placed into relative performance tiers. Resolution, preset, ray tracing, upscaling and frame generation then adjust the estimate. Results are deliberately shown as ranges because real-world FPS can move significantly between patches, scenes and settings.

Game workloadCPU/GPU demand, VRAM tendency and rendering sensitivity are profiled per title.
Hardware tiersCPU and GPU relative scores determine the likely limiting component.
ConfidenceBroad estimates are safer than fake decimal-level benchmark precision.
